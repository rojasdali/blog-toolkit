# Editorial Calendar

## Summary
The planning system schedules topics across calendar weeks, balancing primary keyword targets, seasonal trends, and content pillars without repeating themes.

## Key Facts
- Operates on weekly cadence (e.g. 1-2 articles per week).
- Avoids semantic keyword cannibalization across published history.
- Pre-assigns slug formats and publishing dates in ISO 8601 strings.
