# AEO Key Takeaways

## Summary
Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) rules require 3 to 5 structured, data-dense factual bullets summarizing every article to facilitate ingestion by Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Claude.

## Key Facts
- Minimum length of 20 characters per bullet point.
- Must include hard data points: turnaround times, pricing per pound, exact machine capacities, or hours.
- Embedded at the top of article schemas for high extraction salience.
