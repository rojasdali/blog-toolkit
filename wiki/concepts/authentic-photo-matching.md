# Authentic Photo Matching

## Summary
A deterministic matching engine that pairs generated article topics with authentic, high-resolution location photography based on scene categories, machine types, and tags.

## Key Facts
- Prevents the use of disconnected fantasy AI-generated scenes.
- Matches based on tag intersections (e.g., `folding`, `washers`, `dropoff`, `detergents`).
- Enforces authentic visual DNA including busy store environments and soap suds in front-load washers.
